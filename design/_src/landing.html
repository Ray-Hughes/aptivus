Why it works

Three things a problem list cannot do

Trace

Step or press play and the current line highlights while the panel narrates what just happened — "the condition on line 5 was false, so we skipped its block". Pin any variable and watch it change. Type an expression at >>> and it is evaluated in that frame.

Ask

Stuck on why rather than what? Ask in the pad and the answer arrives grounded in the problem, your current code and your last test run — not the problem in the abstract. Progressive hints sit behind it, then the write-up.

How it works

Five days, one company, no wasted reps

Most prep is fine if you have three months. It is useless if you have five days and a specific name on the calendar.

01

Pick your round

Tell Aptivus the company, the role and the shape of the round. It loads the pack that matches — 45 minutes of part-SQL, part data structures, in underwriting vocabulary, if that is what you are walking into.

02

Practise it for real

Split panes, a countdown, stdin, Run and Submit. Close enough to the pad you will actually sit in that the real thing feels like the fourth time, not the first.

03

Step through what happened

A failing test gets a Trace this case button. You go from a red test to stepping the exact input that broke it, and you learn the bug instead of guessing at it.
